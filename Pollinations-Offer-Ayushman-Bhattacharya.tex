\sectiontitle{Scope \& responsibilities}
The role may include, but is not limited to:
\begin{itemize}[label=\goldbullet]
  \item Product engineering across the Pollinations.ai platform --- developer tooling, SDKs, automation, integrations,
        and release engineering.
  \item Building and maintaining contributor-facing automation and platform services.
  \item Assisting with pull-request reviews, issue triage, testing, deployment support, and documentation.
  \item Community management --- Discord moderation, issue moderation, app-submission review, and contributor onboarding.
  \item Supporting and growing the Pollinations.ai contributor and developer community.
\end{itemize}
